## 👋 About Me

I earned my **Ph.D. from Adelaide University in 2026**, conducting research in the **Empathic Computing Laboratory** on intelligent guidance for physical tasks in augmented and mixed reality.

My work explores how systems can understand **where people are, what they are attending to, and how task steps relate to one another**. I have developed approaches to spatial guidance, attention-aware instruction playback, multimodal task segmentation, and dependency-aware task replay.

I am now extending this foundation toward **embodied agents** that can learn structured task knowledge from human demonstrations and use perception, memory, and action to support real-world tasks.

## 🔬 Research Highlights

- **STAGE — XR Task Recording and Replay** · **OzCHI 2026 (accepted)**  
  A framework for recording, segmenting, authoring, and replaying XR task demonstrations. [Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3847401.3847407)

- **Focus-Aware Task Guidance** · **IEEE ISMAR 2025**  
  Adaptive AR instruction playback driven by gaze and location awareness. [Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/ISMAR67309.2025.00103)

- **Dependency-Aware AR Guidance for Replaying Non-Linear Work** · **ACM CHI EA 2026**  
  Task guidance organized as a dependency graph rather than a fixed linear sequence. [Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772363.3798430)

- **Location-Based Non-Linear Playback of AR Task Recording** · **IEEE VR 2026 Workshop**  
  Location-triggered access to recorded AR task instructions. [Workshop program](https://sites.google.com/view/xrmemory26-ieee-vr2026/main)

## 🧭 Current Research Direction

**From XR task guidance to task-centric embodied intelligence.** I'm particularly interested in:

- **Learning from human demonstrations:** egocentric video, gaze, hand motion, and interaction traces
- **Task understanding:** temporal segmentation, action dependencies, and structured task representations
- **Agent memory and world models:** tracking task progress, state, and long-horizon dependencies
- **Robot learning:** connecting task representations with vision-language-action (VLA) models and robotic execution
- **Human–AI collaboration:** adaptive assistance, shared agency, and interpretable decision-making
