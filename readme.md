I am a researcher working at the intersection of <b>Extended Reality (XR)</b>, <b>Embodied AI</b>, and <b>Intelligent Task Guidance</b>.

I recently completed my PhD research at <b>Adelaide University</b>, where my work focused on
spatial and adaptive XR guidance, multimodal task understanding, and intelligent assistance
using first-person visual and interaction data.

My current interests extend toward <b>Embodied Agents</b>, <b>Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models</b>,
and <b>World Models</b>, particularly how multimodal perception, task structure, memory, and action
can enable agents to understand and interact with complex environments.

<br>
